Dynamic

E1 · Skill fluency
How long does the skill take to execute?
E2 · Act in time
Complete an action within its time window.
E3 · Keep pace
Stay in step with the process over time.

Scoring

Each task has 100 points, divided equally among its milestones with integer rounding. Completed milestones earn their points. Record full-task success separately.

For a timing milestone, record whether it was met and the margin to its deadline where defined. For a sustained action, record the task-specific duration, such as the time spent supporting a lid.
